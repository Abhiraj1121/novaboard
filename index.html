Write, teach and present on a board that opens in one click.

NovaBoard OS is a smartboard-style whiteboard and presentation workspace. Draw with neon pens, annotate PDFs, run a live poll and share your board with a code. No account, no sign-up.

Draw on this board

Four pens, ten shapes and an eraser that keeps up.

Write the way you would on a real board. Switch between pens, pick any colour and set the width with a slider. Undo, redo and erase-all are always within reach.

  • Ball pen, highlighter, calligraphy pen and neon pen
  • Preset swatches, a custom colour picker and a width slider
  • Lines, arrows, rectangles, ellipses and stars
  • 3D cubes, cylinders, spheres and pyramids

Slides, PDFs and pages in one workspace.

Open a PDF as slides and write directly on it. Add pages as you go, drag the thumbnails to reorder them and give each page its own background. When you finish, export the whole board back to a PDF.

  • PDF import with a progress overlay, and PDF export
  • Pan and zoom from 50% to 600% after you unlock the PDF
  • Image upload, sticky notes, copy and paste across pages

Eleven smart tools in one menu.

Everything you reach for during a lesson or a meeting opens on top of the board, without leaving it. Floating widgets stay where you put them.

  • Live Poll
  • Scoreboard
  • Name Picker
  • Group Maker
  • Noise Meter
  • Lesson Agenda
  • QR Code
  • Unit Converter
  • Analog Clock
  • Protractor
  • Screen Shade

Also built in: timer and stopwatch, scientific calculator and a laser pointer.

An assistant that works with a free key.

Add a free API key from Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter in Settings, then open the AI Assistant from the Tools menu. Ask questions, or turn a topic into a quiz, lesson outline, key terms or discussion prompts that land on the board as sticky notes.

  • Send an image of the current page to explain it, summarize it, solve the maths or read the handwriting
  • Keys stay in your browser and are never included in settings exports

The assistant is optional. Nothing is sent unless you use an AI feature, so avoid private student data.

Share a live board with a six-character code.

Choose Start sharing in the Tools menu and read the passcode out loud. On any other device, pick Join live board on the home screen and type it in. The viewer watches your board update as you draw and cannot draw on it.

  • No server and no accounts: boards travel directly between the two browsers
  • Both devices need internet, and some strict school or office networks block peer-to-peer connections

Viewers see your board live

Make it look like your classroom, your team or your brand.

Set your organiser or school name, your own name, a tagline and a profile picture, then choose how the board looks and feels.

  • Eight themes plus a custom accent colour
  • Dark, light or follow-the-system mode
  • Glass blur, corner roundness, interface size and animation controls
  • Export, import and reset your settings

Your work stays safe, and stays yours.

Auto-save with crash recovery

Boards are saved continuously in your browser and restored if the page closes unexpectedly. Recent sessions wait on the home screen.

Your own .novaboard files

Save a whole workspace to a file, back it up, move it to another computer and open it again later.

Smartboard mode on desktop

The Windows and Linux apps bundle their libraries and fonts. Start fullscreen for a classroom display and keep drawing without a connection.

Open it in a browser or install it.

Start in a tab right now, or download the app for the device that sits in front of your class.
